## 8. Summary

### Primary Target Market

**Geography:** Taiwan, Hong Kong, Vietnam, Thailand
**Industry:** E-commerce, Retail (O2O), SaaS/Tech
**Company Size:** 10-200 employees, $100-2,000/month budget
**Channels:** LINE, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Web Widget

### Key Success Factors

1. Native Asia channel support (LINE, Zalo first-class)
2. Self-serve onboarding (< 30 min to first conversation)
3. Transparent, affordable pricing
4. Strong e-commerce integrations
5. Localized content and support

### Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| LINE API changes | Medium | High | Multiple channel fallback |
| Competition from Omnichat | High | Medium | Differentiate on tech/price |
| Slow enterprise adoption | Medium | Medium | Focus on SMB first |
| LLM cost increases | Low | Medium | Multi-model support |

---

*This analysis recommends a focused Asia-first strategy, starting with Taiwan's e-commerce market, leveraging LINE integration as a key differentiator, and expanding regionally before tackling Western markets.*
